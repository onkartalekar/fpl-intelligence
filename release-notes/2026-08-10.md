# 2026-08-10 -- FPL Intelligence's alpha feature set, all in one place

This is the first entry in What's New's dated history -- a snapshot of everything the alpha build already does, migrated from RELEASE_NOTES.md so the tab's history starts in sync with the repo's own docs. Every future entry covers just one day's changes; this one covers the whole alpha feature set built before this tab existed.

- **[Feature]** Decision Center: weekly roll/transfer/chip recommendation across three risk profiles -- Recommended XI, bench, captaincy, a five-gameweek planner, and a full player scoring breakdown -- nothing is a black-box number.
- **[Feature]** My Team / My Profile: no-signup team lookup, manager profile, and a preseason draft squad -- View any public squad by team ID, save your own timezone/risk profile locally, and declare a provisional squad before Gameweek 1's official picks exist.
- **[Feature]** Opt-in deadline email reminder and lookup opt-out -- Recommendations emailed a configurable number of hours before deadline; a manager can also exclude their team from derived recommendations entirely.
- **[Feature]** Player Explorer and Fixtures, with official prices and Fixture Difficulty Ratings -- Search, filter, and sort official prices, positions, and availability; kickoffs and difficulty ratings with prev/next gameweek navigation.
- **[Data]** Confirmed transfer feed sourced from first-party announcements only -- Origin/destination club, announcement timestamp, source URL, and FPL reconciliation status -- rumors are excluded by policy, not just by taste.
- **[Feature]** Model Performance: forecasts frozen before each gameweek, checked against official results after -- Accuracy by horizon for your team, any tracked player, and league-wide history -- never rewritten with hindsight, plus shadow models scored alongside the live one without influencing it.
